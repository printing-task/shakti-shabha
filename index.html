Shakti Sabha (शक्ति सभा) — Union of Peace & Revolution

Shakti Sabha is a grassroots Indian initiative founded by Harshita Mankar (Aaryaveera) working across women's safety, self-defence training, confidential psychosocial counselling, youth leadership, Indian history and heritage, and research-driven public dialogue (2019–Present).

Three Institutional Streams

  • Mission Mardini — Physical and tactical preparedness, Lathi-Kaushal, and self-defence training (2,000+ participants trained).
  • Shakti Bhava & Confidential Counselling — Emotional resilience, trauma-informed listening circles, and support pathways (250+ situations engaged, 13 documented rescues).
  • Shakti Varta & Research — Independent historical inquiry, seminars, and public dialogue (500+ educational sessions, 25,000+ youth reached).
